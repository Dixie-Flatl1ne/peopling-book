# Thought Mash archive cleanup report

## Scope

All 48 published Thought Mash posts were reviewed. The six unpublished Blogger drafts were not included.

- 1 article was previously rewritten and updated live.
- 2 articles received substantial local rewrites and remain pending review.
- 45 remaining published articles received conservative copyedits which preserve the existing voice.
- No additional Blogger posts were changed.

## Archive results

- 28 articles received medium cleanup, generally involving broken formatting, damaged links, extraction residue and sentence repair.
- 15 received light cleanup.
- 2 required no substantive prose change.
- All source images were preserved.
- Nine posts contained videos, slide decks or other embeds omitted by the first text extraction. Their media links were recovered from the original Blogger HTML and restored.
- All 45 archive drafts passed the metadata, image, embedded-media, long-dash and Markdown checks.
- The China reconstruction retained seven sources and passed strict citation verification at 31% sentence coverage.

- **Boston bombers radicalisation memetic cookbook**: Claims about self-radicalisation and a possible CIA-sponsored workshop were preserved and not fact-checked.

- **Franco-British Empire - Alternative History**: Contains sweeping and potentially disputed historical and geopolitical claims about empire, decolonisation, Israel, Suez, and colonial outcomes. These were preserved and require research before publication.
- **Religions explained**: Contains broad and disputed historical and religious claims, including claims about Constantine, early Islam, the Sunni-Shia split, and the causes of later Christian schisms. These were preserved and require research before publication.

- **Demographic decline will hurt Russia the most**: Putin's 154M / 107M population projections, Michal Lee's speculative immigration scenario, and the American Thinker China-Manifest-Destiny claim were preserved as published and not updated.
