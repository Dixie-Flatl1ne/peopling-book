## Why did the tragedy occur? The system was too easy to use

The missiles are straightforward to operate and work as stand-alone weapons. They can function outside a sophisticated networked air defence system.

![](http://static.businessinsider.com/image/53c7faa3eab8eaf44da32dc4-1200/image.jpg)

The system cannot tell the difference between civilian and military-type aircraft based on their transponder signatures alone. To tell the difference between targets, it would need to be interfaced with other weapons systems that can work from additional information.
