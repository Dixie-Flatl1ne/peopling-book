What's with the mainstream German press presenting a different opinion on Ukraine from the US? Are they worried about getting their gas taps turned off, or is it more that they realise Russo-German trade and technology transfers will do more for Germany in the long term than its military alliance with the US?

[German Official Says It Was Wrong to Make Ukraine Pick Between EU and Russia](http://www.newsweek.com/german-official-says-it-was-wrong-make-ukraine-pick-between-eu-and-russia-251038)

[400 Blackwater Mercs Deployed In Ukraine Against Separatists, German Press Reports](http://www.zerohedge.com/news/2014-05-11/400-blackwater-mercs-deployed-ukraine-against-separatists-german-press-reports)

Sure, there are all sorts of 'questionable' things going on that the US may or may not be linked to, such as having US Blackwater mercenaries being transported to fight Russian 'separatists' in UN helicopters:

![](http://d.ibtimes.co.uk/en/full/1378588/ukraine-russia-helicopter.png)

Oh, and the fact that Vice President Joe Biden's son Hunter has [just joined the board of directors of Ukraine's largest gas company](http://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-2627424/VP-Bidens-son-joins-Ukraine-gas-companys-board.html).

![](http://i.dailymail.co.uk/i/pix/2014/05/15/article-2627424-1DDBBF1C00000578-183_634x374.jpg)

But this is just standard [power elite](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Power_Elite) practices that have always existed... what I'm interested in is Germany's rationale. Grown a conscience? Unlikely.

Russian gas in exchange for German-manufactured goods, along with German foreign investment and technology transfers to Russia, is far too valuable to both countries to allow them to sign up to the US-led sanctions programme. The press releases above will be aimed at keeping the German public anti-sanctions by pointing out the murky American influence:

[German exporters are pushing back against economic sanctions on Russia](http://www.economist.com/news/business/21599034-german-exporters-are-pushing-back-against-economic-sanctions-russia-lovers-not-fighters)

[Ukraine crisis: Russia sanctions would hurt Germany's growth](http://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/worldnews/europe/ukraine/10820180/Ukraine-crisis-Russia-sanctions-would-hurt-Germanys-growth.html)
