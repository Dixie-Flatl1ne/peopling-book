After having two nights out drinking last week, I have a question: How do I let future me have more input into decisions present me makes? Apps, behaviour modification, etc. Our brains have evolutionary survival feedback mechanisms which are obsolete and somehow need to be overcome or hijacked.

Brainstorming, I can think of planning and reviewing my calendar for work, but I'm coming up short on finances and health. For finances, you have "you have spent X" notifications; for health, you can have "go for a run" notifications, which are pretty primitive.

What I'm kind of thinking you need is a C3PO whispering predictive behaviour outcome feedback in your ear... e.g. your Google Maps location shows an 80% probability that you are walking to a bar. Given it's a Tuesday, you are likely to consume >5 drinks, which will likely mean you will carb-load and skip exercise for 4 days. This means in 1 week you will be X kg heavier and your life expectancy will have decreased by X days. More medium-to-long-term feedback would be: given you are entering a bar towards the end of a 16-day period of not going to a bar, you are at risk of not completing a 21-day behaviour modification of reducing bar attendance, which will lead to a projected uplift of bar attendance events of X per year, leading to a projected.... you get the point.
