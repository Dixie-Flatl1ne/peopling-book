The main cause of the conflict in Ukraine is a choice it has to make: does it join the European Union, or the Eurasian Union (Russian-led customs union)? The European Union has declared that Ukraine cannot join both. The protestors on the street want Ukraine to join the European Union.

Russia is worried about the integration of Ukraine into the European Union for two reasons:

1. It would effectively kill its Eurasian Union; Ukraine's membership is pivotal.

2. It could lead to Ukrainian membership in NATO. The US has been pushing for this. This would threaten Russia's security.

For most of its history, Ukraine was not a country. It was divided among the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, Austro-Hungarian Empire, Ottomans and Russians.

Crimea, a province of Ukraine where ousted president Viktor Yanukovych has fled to, contains a majority Russian population and was gifted to Ukraine in 1954. Remember Ukraine was part of the USSR so this was more of a transfer of territory within substates, and no one ever conceived that the USSR would eventually break up, otherwise it would never have been gifted!
