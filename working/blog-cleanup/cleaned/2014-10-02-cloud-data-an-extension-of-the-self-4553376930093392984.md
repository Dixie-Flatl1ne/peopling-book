# Cloud data as an extension of the self

Post ID: 4553376930093392984
URL: https://www.thoughtmash.net/2014/10/cloud-data-extension-of-self.html
Published: 2014-10-02T11:53:00+10:00
Labels: 

What if we represent our online profile and data as an extension of ourselves, able to carry out actions as well as be mined for marketing insights?

Amazon is building machine-learning algorithms which suggest items a customer would like to purchase. Google is building AI and algorithms to suggest search answers before a customer even searches for them. I think, from a smartphone perspective, we need to talk in terms of 'assisted decision-making' and 'augmented reality'. The smartphone and an individual's online presence (the sum of their customer data) form a symbiotic relationship with the individual, both suggesting activities and connections and advising on the best way to execute a task being carried out.

Cloud data becomes part of the machinery through which we perceive, decide and act. The question is who controls that machinery, and whether it serves the person whose life produced the data or the company mining it.
