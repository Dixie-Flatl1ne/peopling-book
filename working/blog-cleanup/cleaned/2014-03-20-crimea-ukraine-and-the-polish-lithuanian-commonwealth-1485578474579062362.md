The current crisis in Ukraine can easily be explained with historical maps.

The pro-European area was part of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth. Basically that's all the old Russians (now referred to as Ukrainians) who got conquered by the Poles.

![](http://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f0/Rzeczpospolita2nar.png)

The bits that Poland owned speak Ukrainian; the other bits speak Russian. Ukrainian and Russian were once the same language. They diverged mainly because the people lived in different political entities.

![](http://www.washingtonpost.com/blogs/worldviews/files/2014/01/ukraine-protests-map-by-language-k.jpg)

All the protests and pro-EU stuff is from the part of the country Poland used to own. Next look at the 2010 presidential election results. Yanukovych, the pro-Russian president who had to flee for his life during the protests, won the bits that speak Russian...

![](http://i.cdn.turner.com/cnn/interactive/2014/02/world/ukraine-divided/media/ukraine_map_region_vote.jpg)

Ukrainian land outside of the Polish borders was conquered and settled by Russia from the Ottoman Turks and their Crimean Tatar vassals.

You get the gist.
